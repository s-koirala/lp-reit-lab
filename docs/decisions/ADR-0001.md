# ADR-0001: Open/free-first data architecture

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-05
- **Deciders:** SKIE
- **Related:** [research memo §4, §9, §12](../research_notes/research_market-scoping_2026-06-05.md)

## Context
The lab needs transaction, market, rent, carrying-cost, and location data for
Lincoln Park / North Side Chicago. Sources span open government data, free
research files, and ToS-restricted/paid feeds. The pipeline must respect ToS,
stay reproducible (point-in-time, checksummed), and avoid vendor lock-in.

## Decision
Adopt an **open/free-first** ingestion architecture:

- **Transactions / characteristics / assessment:** Cook County Assessor Socrata
  (`wvhk-k5uv` sales, `uzyt-m557` assessed values, `x54s-btds` characteristics,
  `nj4t-kc8j` parcel universe), IL IDOR MyDec.
- **Market / supply-demand:** Zillow / Redfin / Realtor research files; FHFA HPI
  (repeat-sales); DePaul IHS composition-controlled submarket index; Chicago
  building permits.
- **Rents:** Zillow ZORI, HUD FMR/SAFMR, Census ACS B25064/B25063.
- **Carrying costs:** Cook County Treasurer; EIA / ComEd / Peoples Gas / City of
  Chicago; modeled insurance.
- **Location:** EPA National Walkability Index (free) over Walk Score (paid,
  caching-barred); CTA GTFS; OSM Overpass + Chicago Park District; CPS attendance
  boundaries + ISBE Illinois Report Card.
- **Join spine:** Parcel Universe lat/lon → block group / tract / community area;
  GTFS stop distance; OSM amenity radius; CPS boundary point-in-polygon.

Paid / ToS sources (MLS-MRED, RentCast, ATTOM, Google Places, GreatSchools, Walk
Score) are catalogued as optional enrichment, evaluated case-by-case, never
load-bearing in the reproducible pipeline.

## Consequences
- (+) Reproducible, license-clean, no vendor lock-in; keyless scriptable endpoints.
- (+) Point-in-time integrity via `data/_manifest.json` checksums + snapshot dates.
- (−) Structural gaps remain — condo bed/bath/sqft (absent from CCAO), rental
  days-to-lease (MLS-gated), HOA/special-assessment history (per-listing). Each is
  modeled as an explicit range with uncertainty until an MLS/MRED agreement is
  evaluated (future ADR).
- (−) Some research figures need programmatic re-pull (provider 403 anti-bot).

## Alternatives considered
- **MLS/MRED-first:** richest condo + days-to-lease data, but paid, access-gated,
  and redistribution-restricted; deferred to a future paid-acquisition ADR.
- **Scraping listing portals:** prohibited by ToS; rejected.
